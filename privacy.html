Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 27, 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how our TodayQuiz Application handles information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Anonymous Authentication: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a unique identifier (UID) for your device so we can save your progress without requiring your name or email.

TodayQuiz Data: We store your quiz scores, completion times, and streak data to provide the core gameplay experience.

Notifications: We use native app notifications to send daily reminders. You can opt-out of these at any time via your device settings.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to:

  • Maintain your game progress across sessions.
  • Send daily "New TodayQuiz Ready" reminders.
  • Analyze aggregate (non-identifiable) performance to improve quiz difficulty.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, and notifications. Their privacy policy can be found at Firebase Privacy & Security.

4. Data Deletion (2026 Compliance)

Since we use anonymous accounts, your data is linked to your specific device. If you delete the app, your anonymous profile may become inaccessible. To request a manual deletion of your score data associated with your UID, please contact us below.
