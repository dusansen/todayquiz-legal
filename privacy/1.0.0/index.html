Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 9, 2026 — Version 1.0.0

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the Today Quiz - Daily Trivia Game app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Anonymous Authentication. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. When you first open the app, a random unique identifier (UID) is created automatically for your device — no name, email address, or any personal information is required or collected.

Quiz Results. We store only your quiz results (e.g., answers submitted and score for each daily quiz) linked to your anonymous UID. No other activity, usage analytics, or device data is collected.

Push Notifications. The app will request your permission to send push notifications. If you grant permission, we use it solely to notify you when a new daily quiz is ready to play. We do not send marketing messages or share your notification token with third parties. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To save and display your quiz results within the app.
  • To send you a daily notification when your new quiz is available (only if you have granted permission).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC) for anonymous authentication and database storage. Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's privacy standards. You can review their policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your quiz results are linked to your anonymous UID, not to any personal identity. If you uninstall the app, access to your anonymous account is lost. To request deletion of quiz result data associated with your UID, please contact us using the details below and include your anonymous UID (visible in app settings).

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Because we use anonymous authentication, no personal data is collected from any user regardless of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
