Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026 — Version 1.2.0

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the Today Quiz: Daily Brain Trivia app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

What changed in version 1.2.0
  • The data behind features added since the previous version: coins, XP, levels, avatars and achievements, live quizzes, invites, ads you can watch for coins, feedback and question reports, and reminders.
  • Services this policy did not name before: Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics), Sentry (crash reports) and Firebase App Check (app integrity).
  • New sections on what other players can see, where your data is processed, how long we keep it, and your rights.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and ads now start only after you have accepted this policy.

1. Who We Are

The app is developed and run by the developer of Today Quiz (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For any question or request about your data, contact us at contact@todayquizapp.com.

2. Information We Collect

Playing as a Guest. You can play without an account. Firebase Anonymous Authentication gives your installation of the app a random guest ID, and we store your quiz results and your streak under that ID. No name, email address or other contact detail is required or collected. Guests do not earn coins or XP and do not appear on leaderboards.

Signing In with Apple or Google. You may sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication. We then receive from the provider and store the following information linked to your account:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name (if provided by the provider; Apple allows you to hide your name)
  • A unique identifier issued by the provider (Apple ID or Google Account ID)

We do not receive or store your password. Authentication is handled entirely by Apple or Google. If you sign in while playing as a guest and a new account is created, your guest progress becomes part of it. If you sign in to an account that already exists, only your recent daily quiz result is moved to that account (when it has none for that day); your other guest results are deleted, together with the guest profile.

Username. Signed-in players choose a username. It is stored in our database and is publicly visible to other players (see Section 4). Your username also serves as your invite code. Please do not include personal information in your username.

Quiz Results. For each quiz you play — daily, past, themed and live quizzes — we store the quiz and date, your score, your share of correct answers, your results per category and which lifelines you used. We use them to show your history and statistics, to calculate streaks, rewards and leaderboards, and to keep your progress in sync between devices.

Coins, XP, Levels, Avatars, Achievements and Streaks. For signed-in players we store your coin balance, XP and level, the avatars you own and the one you use (and when you last changed it), your achievements and the progress counters behind them, your current and longest streak, and what you spent coins on (avatars, the 50:50 lifeline, streak restores and live quiz entry fees). We also keep records that stop a reward from being paid twice, such as which results and ad rewards have already been credited.

Live Quizzes. When you register for a live quiz, we store your registration, the username and avatar you had at that moment, the entry fee you paid, each answer you give together with the time our server received it and the time your device measured, your points, your final rank and any prize you won.

Invites. When a new player enters your invite code, we record on their account that you invited them, and on yours how many friends you have invited and the rewards paid. To stop the same person from claiming an invite bonus twice, we keep a one-way hash (a scrambled fingerprint) of the email address of the account that claimed it, even after that account is deleted. The app never reads your contacts — you choose where to share your code.

Groups. If you create or join a group, we store the group name, its invite code, its members (their account IDs and usernames) and their scores within the group. Group members see each other's usernames and scores; their apps also receive each member's account ID (a random code that is not shown on screen) to manage the group.

Feedback, Question Reports and Messages. When you send feedback or report a question, we store your message (up to 1,000 characters), the type and reason you chose, the quiz and question concerned, the app language and your account or guest ID. We do not ask for your email address this way. If we reply, the reply appears in the Messages inbox in the app and is stored with your account. Please do not include personal details in your messages.

Reminders and Notifications. With your permission, the app shows reminders: a daily reminder at the time you choose, reminders to keep your streak going, and reminders for live quizzes you asked to be reminded about. They are scheduled on your device — we do not collect a push notification token, and no reminder data leaves your device. We do not send marketing messages. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your device settings at any time.

Usage Statistics. After you have accepted this policy, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: the screens you open and actions such as starting or finishing a quiz, using a lifeline, buying an avatar, watching an ad for coins or sharing a result. They come with technical data (app version, device model, operating system and language), an app-instance identifier, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. We do not link usage statistics to your name, email address, username or account ID. On Android, and on iOS if you allow tracking, Google Analytics may also receive your device's advertising identifier.

Crash and Error Reports. After you have accepted this policy, if the app crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report to Sentry. A report contains the error and where it happened, the app version, device model and operating system, the screens and taps that led up to it, and your account or guest ID so we can investigate problems affecting your account. We do not attach your name, email address or username, and we remove your IP address from the report. For a small sample of sessions, the app also sends timing measurements that help us find slow screens.

App Integrity. To protect our servers from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck (iOS) to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device. These services check device-integrity signals; we receive only a token saying whether the check passed.

Advertising Data. The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. Ads start only after you have accepted this policy and, where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), after you have answered Google's consent form. These advertising partners may automatically collect certain information from your device in order to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected directly by the advertising SDKs under each partner's own privacy policy and is not stored by us. When you choose to watch an ad for coins, the app also sends your account ID and a one-time claim number to Google AdMob with the ad request, so that the reward can be matched to your account.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iOS 14 and later, the app will present Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you grant permission, data from this app may be used to track you across apps and websites for targeted advertising purposes. If you deny permission, only non-personalized ads will be shown. You can update this choice at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. How We Use Your Data and Why

  • To run the app and your account — sign-in, saving and syncing your results, streaks, coins, XP, levels, avatars and achievements, leaderboards, groups, live quizzes and invites. Legal basis: necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To keep the game fair and secure — timing live quiz answers on our server, preventing duplicate rewards and cheating, and checking app integrity. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a fair and secure game.
  • To fix problems and improve the app — crash reports and usage statistics. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in an app that works and keeps getting better. You can object at any time (Section 10).
  • To answer your feedback and question reports and to correct quiz content. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to you and keeping the quizzes accurate.
  • To show you reminders. Legal basis: your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in the app's settings or your device settings.
  • To show ads that keep the app free. Legal basis: our legitimate interest for non-personalized ads; your consent for personalized ads (given through Google's consent form where required, and through the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS).
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. What Other Players Can See

  • Daily and monthly leaderboards (top 1,000): your username, avatar and points; the monthly leaderboard also shows the days you played and your change in rank.
  • All-time XP leaderboard (top 250): your username, avatar, XP and level.
  • Streak leaderboard (top 250): your username, avatar, current streak and longest streak.
  • Group leaderboards: members of your groups see your username, avatar and scores.
  • Live quizzes: during and after a live quiz, the top 50 players are shown with their username, avatar, score, number of correct answers and rank. These leaderboards are published as files on the public internet (see Section 8 for how long they are kept).

You can hide yourself from the global leaderboards in your Profile (Global Leaderboard → Hidden). This removes you from the all-time XP and streak leaderboards straight away and from the daily leaderboard at its next update. Points you scored before hiding stay on the monthly leaderboards until those months are cleared. Hiding does not apply to group or live quiz leaderboards.

Guests do not appear on leaderboards. Your coin balance, achievements, statistics and email address are never shown to other players. If you share a level-up, achievement or live quiz result card, it shows your username and avatar wherever you choose to post it.

6. Advertising Choices

By default, personalized advertising may be served based on your advertising identifier. You have the following controls:

  • iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier.
  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of ads personalization or reset your advertising ID.
  • You may also manage ad preferences through Google's Ad Settings.

Where Google's consent form is shown, the choice you make there is stored on your device and applies to every ad request from the app. Opting out of personalized ads limits ad targeting but does not remove advertisements from the app.

7. Where Your Data Is Processed

Our database (Cloud Firestore) is stored in the European Union. The server functions that process your requests run in Google Cloud data centers in the United States, and live quiz files are stored there as well. Sentry stores crash reports in the European Union (Germany). Google Analytics, Google AdMob and our advertising partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, these providers rely on safeguards recognized under EU law, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account data (profile, results, coins, XP, avatars, achievements, streaks, group memberships and messages): until you delete your account.
  • Guest data: until you ask us to delete it. We do not currently delete inactive guest profiles automatically.
  • Live quizzes: the details of each answer are deleted about 30 days after the quiz ends; your final result stays with your account. The published leaderboard files are deleted after about 400 days, and the final top 50 of each live quiz is kept in our database as the permanent record of the event.
  • Leaderboards: daily leaderboards are kept for about three days and monthly leaderboards for the current and the previous month; the all-time XP and streak leaderboards are rebuilt every day.
  • Feedback and question reports: kept so we can act on them and improve the quizzes. They are stored separately from your account and are not removed when you delete it — ask us and we will delete them.
  • Invite-bonus email hash: kept indefinitely, only to prevent repeat bonus claims.
  • Usage statistics: kept by Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for no longer than 90 days.
  • Server logs, which may include your account ID or username: kept for a limited period (30 days by default).

9. Deleting Your Data

Signed-in players — in-app account deletion. You can delete your account directly in the app at any time:

  1. Open your Profile (tap your profile card on the home screen).
  2. Tap Delete Account and review what will be deleted.
  3. Confirm deletion when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. It permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in account, email address and display name
  • Your profile: username, coins, XP and level, avatars, achievements, streaks and messages
  • All your quiz results and scores, including live quiz results
  • Your group memberships (a group of which you were the last member is deleted)
  • Your entries on the daily, all-time XP and streak leaderboards, and your row on the leaderboards of live quizzes held in the last 30 days

Some data may remain after deletion: your username and points on the monthly leaderboards until those months are cleared (within about two months); your row on the published leaderboards of live quizzes held more than 30 days earlier; feedback and question reports you sent, which we delete on request; the invite-bonus email hash; and, if you invited friends, the now unused ID of your deleted account on their accounts. Your username becomes available to other players again straight away. Any copies in backups are deleted within 30 days.

Guests. Uninstalling the app does not delete guest data from our servers. To have it deleted, send us a request from the app via Settings → Send feedback — it reaches us together with your guest ID, which is how we find your data — or email us.

Additionally:

  • If you signed in with Apple, you can revoke app access via Settings → [Your Name] → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can revoke app access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Revoking provider access does not automatically delete your data from our database. Please use the in-app deletion feature above. If you experience any difficulty completing the deletion, contact us at contact@todayquizapp.com.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live — including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection — you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (you can change your username in the app yourself);
  • have your data deleted (see Section 9);
  • restrict how we use your data, or object to uses based on our legitimate interests, such as usage statistics and crash reports;
  • receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time — for reminders and tracking in your device settings, and for personalized ads as described in Section 6 — without affecting processing that took place before.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@todayquizapp.com. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Croatia the Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr), in Serbia the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs), or the authority of the EU country where you live.

11. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Guests do not provide personal information. However, users who sign in with Apple or Google will have their email address stored by us. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored on Google Cloud with access limited to what is needed to run the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to fix problems quickly when we find them.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date and version at the top show the latest change. When we make significant changes, the app asks you to review and accept the new version before you continue playing. Earlier versions remain available on our website.
